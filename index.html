The Fox and Monsters

Gear up at the camp, choose your spells at the spellbook, then step through the portal with your fox. Every five waves is a new floor, and clearing it opens a portal home. Monsters drop gold for the trader and blue shards that level you up. Every fifth wave, a portal can take you back to camp.
